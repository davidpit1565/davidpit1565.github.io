Questions

Which iPhones does it work on?

iPhone 11 and later, running iOS 26. Zoom range and lens buttons adapt to your model.

Is it really free?

Yes. There is no subscription and no paywall.

Does it use the internet or AI in the cloud?

No. The coach runs on your iPhone using Apple's on-device frameworks. Kelvo works offline and collects no data.

Can I open it from the Camera Control button?

On iPhone 16 and later: Settings → Camera → Camera Control, then choose Kelvo (when available).

Where do my photos go?

Straight to your Photos library. Kelvo can only add photos; it cannot read your existing ones.
