Questions

Good to know.

Which iPhones does it work on?

iPhone 11 and later, running iOS 26. Lens buttons and the zoom range adapt to your model.

Is it really free?

Yes. No subscription, no paywall, no ads.

Does it use AI in the cloud?

No. The coach runs on your iPhone with Apple's on-device frameworks. Ocello makes no network requests and collects no data.

What does “Enhanced” mean?

Beyond the lens limit, Ocello crops and carefully resamples the image, and labels it. Far beyond that, fine detail is approximate, and the app says so instead of pretending.

Where do my photos go?

Straight to your Photos library, with add-only permission.
