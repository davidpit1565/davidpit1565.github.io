Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Short version

Ocello does not collect, store or share personal data. Everything the app does happens on your iPhone.

What the app uses

  • Camera to show the viewfinder and take photos.
  • Photo library (add only) to save the photos you take. Ocello cannot read your existing photos.
  • Motion sensors to show a level and detect shaking.

Analysis for coaching (faces, framing) is computed on the device and is never sent anywhere. Ocello has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no third-party SDKs, and makes no network requests.

Purchases

Ocello is free and has no in-app purchases.

Contact

If you email support, we use your address and message only to reply, and delete them after 12 months. You have the right to access, correct or erase that data and to complain to the Belgian Data Protection Authority (gegevensbeschermingsautoriteit.be).
