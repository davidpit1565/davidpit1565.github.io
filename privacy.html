Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Short version

Kelvo does not collect, store or share personal data. Everything the app does happens on your iPhone.

What the app uses

  • Camera to show the viewfinder and take photos.
  • Photo library (add only) to save the photos you take. Kelvo cannot read your existing photos.
  • Motion sensors to show a level and detect shaking.

Analysis for coaching (faces, framing) is computed on the device and is never sent anywhere. Kelvo has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no third-party SDKs.

Purchases

Purchases are handled entirely by Apple. Kelvo never sees your payment details.

Contact

If you email support, we use your address and message only to reply, and delete them after 12 months. You have the right to access, correct or erase that data and to complain to the Belgian Data Protection Authority (gegevensbeschermingsautoriteit.be).
