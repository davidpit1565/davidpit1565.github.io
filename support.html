Common questions

Why is a photo labelled “Enhanced”?
Beyond what the camera sensor can capture, Kelvo crops and sharpens the image. It does not invent detail.

How do I restore my purchase?
Open Settings in Kelvo and tap Restore Purchases.

Camera Control button
iPhone 16 and later: Settings → Camera → Camera Control, then choose Kelvo.
