Common questions

Why is a photo labelled “Enhanced”?
Beyond what the camera sensor can capture, Kelvo crops and sharpens the image. It does not invent detail. At the highest zoom levels fine detail is approximate, and the app says so.

The camera is black.
Check Settings → Kelvo → Camera is on.

Photos aren't saving.
Check Settings → Kelvo → Photos is set to “Add Photos Only” or more.

Camera Control button
iPhone 16 and later: Settings → Camera → Camera Control, then choose Kelvo (when available).
